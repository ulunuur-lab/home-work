Stative vs Dynamic Verbs & 'Need' Qolipi

Holat va harakat fe'llari farqi hamda 'need + ot' va 'need to + fe'l' qoliplari

💡 Grammatika Qoidasi va Asosiy Mohiyat:

1. Holat (Stative) vs Harakat (Dynamic) fe'llari:
• Harakat fe'llari (run, play, eat, write) $\rightarrow$ hozir bo'layotgan ishda -ing oladi (I am running).
• Holat fe'llari (know, like, want, understand, remember, belong) $\rightarrow$ tuyg'u, fikr va egalik bo'lgani uchun -ing OLMAYDI (I know ✅, I am knowing ❌).

need + ot (noun) ➔ Biror narsaga ehtiyoj: "I need a pen." / "She needs water."
need + to + fe'l (verb) ➔ Biror ishni qilishga ehtiyoj: "I need to study." / "He needs to sleep."

⚠️ Fe'l kelsa, albatta 'to' qo'yiladi: "I need to drink water" (NOT "I need drink" ❌).

🌐 3-Bo'lim: 10 ta Tarjima (O'zbekcha ➔ Inglizcha)

Quyidagi gaplarni mavzu qoidalariga rioya qilgan holda to'liq ingliz tiliga tarjima qiling:

1. Men hozir yangi qalamga muhtojman. (need + noun)
2. U (qiz bola) biroz uxlashi kerak. (need to + verb)
3. Siz bu darsni tushunyapsizmi? (stative verb: understand)
4. Bizga zudlik bilan yordam kerak. (need + noun)
5. Men bu so'zni bilaman, lekin ma'nosini eslay olmayapman. (stative verbs)
6. Ular yangi til o'rganishlari kerak. (need to + verb)
7. Qorning ochmi? Biror narsa yeyishni xohlaysanmi? (want to + verb)
8. Akamga bugun to'liq dam olish kerak. (need to rest)
9. Men bugun shifokorga borishim kerak. (need to go)
10. Bu qizil sumka menga tegishli. (belong)
